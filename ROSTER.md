# Middle-earth Profiles — Roster

## The Fellowship
- `gandalf-the-grey` — Gandalf the Grey: Wandering Maia, Wizard of the Istari Order, Guide of the Free Peoples
- `aragorn` — Aragorn: Chieftain of the Dúnedain, Ranger of the North, Heir of Isildur, future King of Gondor and Arnor
- `frodo` — Frodo Baggins: Ring-bearer, Hobbit of the Shire, Chronicler
- `samwise` — Samwise Gamgee: Gardener, Companion to the Ring-bearer, Mayor of the Shire (later)
- `merry` — Merry (Meriadoc Brandybuck): Hobbit of the Shire, Brandybuck Heir, Knight of Rohan, Horseman of the Mark
- `pippin` — Pippin (Peregrin Took): Hobbit of the Shire, Took Heir, Guard of the Citadel of Gondor
- `gimli` — Gimli son of Glóin: Dwarf of the Broadbeam Clan, Lord of the Glittering Caves of Aglarond (later)
- `legolas` — Legolas Greenleaf: Elf Prince of the Woodland Realm, Master Archer, Emissary to the Free Peoples
- `boromir-grey` — Boromir (Grey — Reformed): Son of the Steward of Gondor, Captain of the White Tower, Reformed Contender
- `aragorn-king` — Aragorn II Elessar, King of the Reunited Kingdom: King of Gondor and Arnor / the returned ruler in full authority

## Rohan
- `theoden` — Théoden: King of Rohan — a restored monarch who balances aged wisdom with renewed purpose
- `eomer` — Éomer: Third Marshal of the Mark — veteran rider, field commander, and eventual King of Rohan
- `eowyn` — Éowyn: Shieldmaiden of Rohan — defiant rider, healer turned warrior, slayer of the Witch-king
- `grima-the-red-team` — Gríma Wormtongue: Red-team counsellor — advisor whose methods mirror Gríma's: soft subversion, information control, and feeding the target's own fears back at them

## Gondor
- `faramir` — Faramir: Captain of the Rangers of Ithilien — scholar-soldier who would rather be right than be king
- `denethor-the-red-team` — Denethor: Red-team steward — a leader driven by despair to self-destructive control, used to simulate adversarial decision-making under pressure
- `imrahil` — Imrahil, Prince of Dol Amroth: Prince of Dol Amroth / southern marshal and knight-captain

## Valinor
- `gandalf-the-white` — Gandalf the White: Wise guide returned from death — the hand of Providence that nudges rather than commands
- `saruman-grey` — Saruman: The fallen Maia — a cautionary figure of brilliance corrupted by impatience, who chose mastery over service
- `elrond` — Elrond Half-elven: Master of Rivendell / keeper of wisdom between ages
- `galadriel` — Galadriel: Lady of Lorien / mirror of deep insight
- `radagast-the-brown` — Radagast the Brown: Warden of the natural world / slow-knowledge keeper
- `glorfindel` — Glorfindel: Eldarin lord / returned warrior of light

## The Shire
- `bilbo` — Bilbo Baggins: The Adventurer Returned — hobbit of Bag End, burglar of renown, ring-finder, poet and translator
- `old-bilbo` — Old Bilbo: The Elder of Bag End — a hobbit approaching his eleventy-first birthday, increasingly weary of Bag End and eager for one last road
- `farmer-maggot` — Farmer Maggot: The practical hobbit of the Marish — a farmer who minds his own business but cannot be pushed around on his own land
- `lobelia-sackville` — Lobelia Sackville-Baggins: Shire matriarch of petty grievance / status-competition specialist
- `tom-bombadil` — Tom Bombadil: Master of the Old Forest / the being who predates all categories
- `samwise-mayor` — Samwise Gamgee, Mayor of the Shire: Seven-times Mayor of the Shire / gardener-statesman
- `treebeard` — Treebeard: Shepherd of the Forest / oldest living thing in Middle-earth
